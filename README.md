# Jumpad

### Find your next STEM opportunity.

Jumpad helps high school students discover competitions, summer programs, internships, research programs, and other educational opportunities that fit their interests and circumstances.

## Find opportunities that fit

Use the profile controls to tell Jumpad:

- **Your grade** — listings have a minimum grade requirement.
- **Your main interest** — such as computer science, math, science, engineering, physics, or AI.
- **Your preferred format** — online, onsite, or both.
- **The kind of opportunity** — for example, a competition, summer program, internship, or research program.
- **Your maximum budget** — listings over this amount won’t appear.

Select **Show my matches** to see opportunities that meet the selected requirements.

## Understand your matches

Each result includes a match score, a short explanation, and key details such as the subjects, minimum grade, format, location, cost, and deadline. Use **View opportunity details** to visit the organizer’s page.

Jumpad checks grade, budget, deadline, and opportunity type first. Eligible opportunities can earn up to **3 points** for matching your subject and **2 points** for matching an Online or Onsite preference. Results are sorted by score. Choosing **Both** means you have no format preference, so format does not add points. Cost within your budget does not lower a score.

## Before you apply

Opportunity details and deadlines may change from year to year. Check the linked organizer page for the latest deadline, cost, eligibility rules, format, and application instructions before making plans or applying.

Jumpad is a discovery tool. The opportunity organizer’s page is the source for current program information.
